# Code of Conduct

We aim for a safe and welcoming place to work.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient, assume good intent, give and accept constructive
feedback, and keep discussions on the topic.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing other people's private
information, or spam.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules,
and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, open a private security advisory or contact
the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile.
